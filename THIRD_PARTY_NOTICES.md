# Third-party software and data

Original workflow code is released under the MIT License. Python dependencies
are installed from their upstream distributions under their own licenses.

This release contains no EMC/GROMACS executable, third-party force-field
distribution, model weights, credentials or compute account. Obtain EMC assets
under EMC's terms and GROMACS from its official release or a reviewed site
installation. The managed installer records source URLs, versions, digests and
whether upstream regression tests were executed.

Small original coordinate/topology fixtures are supplied for acceptance checks
and identified in the science-core manifest. Retained case summaries and audit
responses accompany the demonstrator. Full trajectories, private cluster logs
and third-party paper PDFs remain separate from the source release.
